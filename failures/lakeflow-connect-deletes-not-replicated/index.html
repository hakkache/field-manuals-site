Deleted rows never disappear from the destination.

Failure family · Schema Lakeflow Connect · part 06 · failure 12 of 16
Symptom
Rows deleted at the source remain in the destination indefinitely, and a dropped source table is still present in Databricks.
Cause
Two separate causes with the same appearance. Cursor-based connectors cannot detect deletes — a deleted row simply stops appearing rather than generating an event. And when a source table is deleted the destination table is not automatically deleted; you must remove it manually.
Fix
Confirm your connector's delete semantics before designing around them, because CDC-based connectors detect deletes and cursor-based ones do not. Where deletes matter and the connector cannot detect them, schedule periodic full refreshes or reconcile against a source count. Add a manual cleanup step when source tables are decommissioned — nothing will prompt you.

Check first — If deletes matter to your business logic, and for anything financial or regulatory they do, verify the mechanism for your specific source rather than assuming every managed connector does native CDC.
