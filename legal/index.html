Privacy & terms.

What this site collects, what the mailing list is for, and the terms that come with the manuals.

Short, because there is not much to say. This page covers what the site collects, what the mailing list is for, and the terms that come with the manuals.

What this site collects

The site sets no cookies of its own and shows no advertising. Two third parties are involved in serving it:

  • Cloudflare Web Analytics measures page views and referrers. It is privacy-first: it uses no cookies, no localStorage and no fingerprinting, and it does not track you across sites or build a profile of you. Because nothing is stored on your device, no consent banner is required.
  • Google Fonts serves the typefaces. Google receives the request and your IP address as part of delivering them.

Nothing else runs on these pages. No data collected here is sold, rented or shared for advertising.

The mailing list

The notification form hands your address to Gumroad, which hosts the subscriber list under its own privacy policy. It is used for one thing: telling you when a manual is released. It is not shared, rented or sold.

Every email carries an unsubscribe link. You can also write to hakkache.mohamed@gmail.com to be removed, to ask for a copy of what is held about you, or to have it deleted, and it will be actioned promptly.

Purchases and downloads

All downloads and purchases are handled by Gumroad, which acts as the merchant of record. Gumroad's terms and privacy policy apply to the transaction, and it is responsible for collecting and remitting any applicable sales tax or VAT. Payment card details are never seen by, sent to, or stored on this site.

Prices are shown in US dollars. The Auto Loader Field Manual is free and is intended to stay free.
