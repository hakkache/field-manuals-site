About

An independent
publishing project.

The Databricks Guide Series is built as an independent technical publishing project focused on turning complex Databricks concepts into structured, practical engineering references.

Each manual is researched against current Databricks documentation, tested against technical analysis and hands-on experimentation, and then organised around the one idea that makes the feature predictable. Failure modes are drawn from documented behaviour, platform limits and production-oriented patterns — presented as diagnosis and recovery rather than as a list of things that could theoretically go wrong.

Questions

Before you ask.

What is a Field Manual?

A focused technical reference on one Databricks topic, built around a single organising idea and then sixteen documented failure modes with symptom, cause and fix. It also includes architecture, production patterns, limits, a checklist and a cheat sheet — roughly forty pages of engineering reference rather than tutorial prose.

How is this different from Databricks documentation?

The documentation tells you what a feature does and how to configure it. A Field Manual tells you what happens when it's wrong: how to recognise the failure, why it happened, what to check first, and how to recover. Both are useful, and they answer different questions.

Is Auto Loader really free?

Yes. The complete 47-page Auto Loader Field Manual is available for free. Not a sample chapter, not a preview — the whole manual.

Are the other guides available?

Not yet. They are being released progressively. Eighteen manuals are written and the catalogue is scoped to 51 — the rest are marked Coming soon or Planned so you can see exactly where the series stands.

Will there be bundles?

Yes. Individual Field Manuals, category collections and a complete library are planned. Pricing will be published when each product goes live.

When will paid guides be available?

Paid Field Manuals will be released progressively as the catalogue launches. Auto Loader is available today and stays free.

Are these official Databricks publications?

No. They are independent educational publications and are not affiliated with or endorsed by Databricks.
